Delete your Social Manager data

LAST UPDATED 8 NOV 2026 · BRANDON T. BANDE

Social Manager is my personal post-scheduling app and only connects to accounts I own. If you want its access removed, or any data it stored deleted, follow the two steps below.

1. Remove its access

This stops Social Manager from using the account straight away.

  • Facebook: open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Social Manager and choose Remove.
  • Instagram: open Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, find Social Manager and choose Remove.
  • Threads: open Settings → Account → Website permissions, find Social Manager and choose Remove.
  • LinkedIn: open Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services and remove Social Manager.
  • X: open Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps and revoke access.
  • Bluesky: open Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords and delete the one made for Social Manager.
  • dev.to: open Settings → Extensions → DEV Community API Keys and revoke the key made for Social Manager.

The names of these menus sometimes change. If you can't find one, search the platform's settings for “apps” or “permissions”.

2. Ask me to delete what is stored

Send “Delete my Social Manager data” to me on LinkedIn or X (@Rogue0015), and say which platform and account it is about.

What gets deleted

  • the sign-in token for that account;
  • the account's ID and display name;
  • posts and statistics linked to that account;
  • activity log notes that mention it.

How long it takes

Within 30 days of your message. I will reply to confirm once it is done.

For everything Social Manager stores and why, read the privacy policy.
