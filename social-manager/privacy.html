Social Manager privacy policy

LAST UPDATED 8 NOV 2026 · BRANDON T. BANDE

Social Manager is a personal tool and I am its only user. It posts to my own accounts, keeps its data on my own computer, and never sells or shares that data.

What Social Manager is

Social Manager is an app I built to plan, schedule and publish posts to my own accounts (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky and dev.to) from one calendar, and to turn posts from this blog into a posting schedule.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up or sign in, and it only acts on accounts I own. It runs on my own computer and can only be reached from that computer.

This policy explains what data Social Manager handles, including data it receives from Meta (Facebook, Instagram and Threads), how that data is used and protected, and how to have it deleted.

What data it handles

Sign-in tokens

When I connect one of my accounts, the platform gives Social Manager an access token that lets it post on my behalf. Tokens are kept in a private settings file on my computer. They are never shown in the app, written to logs, or sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.

Basic account details

The account's ID and display name, as the platform returns them. Social Manager uses these to post as that account and to show me which account is connected.

Posts I write

The text and images of my posts, when they are scheduled, which platforms they go to, the link to each published post, and the reason if a post fails.

Statistics for my own posts

Totals such as views, reactions, comments and reshares on posts I published, read from the platform or from analytics files I export myself. These are numbers only: Social Manager does not record who reacted or what they wrote.

An activity log

Short notes such as “Posted to LinkedIn” so I can see what happened and when. Only the newest 1,000 notes are kept.

AI drafts

Draft posts written for me by Google's Gemini from my own ideas and blog posts, and the style settings (“personas”) I use for them. I read and edit every draft before I post it myself.

What it does not collect

  • Nothing about other people: no profiles, friends, followers, messages or posts of anyone else.
  • No passwords. Signing in happens on the platform's own website.
  • No advertising, tracking cookies or analytics inside the app.

How the data is used

Only to:

  • publish, edit and delete posts on my own accounts, when I ask it to;
  • show me whether each account is connected and whether each post went out;
  • show me statistics for my own posts;
  • write draft posts from my own ideas and blog posts, for me to review.

The data is not used for advertising or profiling, is not sold, and is not combined with data from other sources. Data received from Meta is used only for the purposes above, as Meta's Platform Terms require.

Who it is shared with

No one. Social Manager does not sell, rent or give data to anyone, and it uses no outside servers or service providers to store it. The only data that leaves my computer is what the job needs: a post is sent to the platform I publish it on, and each request to a platform carries that platform's own token.

One exception: when I ask the app to draft a post, the idea or blog post text I give it is sent to Google's Gemini API, which writes the drafts. Nothing received from Meta, LinkedIn or the other platforms is sent to it. On Google's free tier, Google may use what it receives to improve its products.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Everything is stored on my own computer, in a local database and a private settings file that only my user account can read.
  • The app only accepts connections from that same computer; it cannot be reached from the internet.
  • All connections to Meta, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky and dev.to use encrypted HTTPS.
  • Reports I download from the app are saved on my own computer.
  • The app's source code is public, but tokens and keys are never part of it.

If this changes, for example if the app moves to a hosted server, I will update this policy before the change happens.

How long it is kept

  • Sign-in tokens: until they expire (LinkedIn and Meta tokens last about 60 days) or until I remove them, whichever comes first.
  • Posts and statistics: until I delete them in the app.
  • Activity log: the newest 1,000 notes; older ones are deleted automatically.

Deleting your data

Because I am the only person who uses Social Manager, the only account data it holds is mine. If you believe it holds data about you, or you want to remove its access to an account, you can:

  1. Remove its access in the platform's settings. On Facebook look for Apps and websites; on Instagram and Threads look for Website permissions; then remove Social Manager. Its token stops working straight away.
  2. Ask me to delete what is stored by sending “Delete my Social Manager data” to me on LinkedIn or X (@Rogue0015). Within 30 days I will delete the token, account details, posts and statistics linked to that account, and reply to confirm.

Step-by-step instructions are on the data deletion page.

Children

Social Manager is not meant for children and does not collect data about them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Social Manager is run by Brandon T. Bande, Gweru, Zimbabwe. For any question about this policy or your data, message me on LinkedIn or X (@Rogue0015).
