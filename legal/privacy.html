Privacy policy

LAST UPDATED 8 NOV 2026 · BRANDON T. BANDE

This is a personal website. You don't need an account to use it, I don't use cookies or ads, and I only count visits in a way that doesn't identify you.

What this policy covers

This policy covers rogue0015.github.io: the homepage, the blog, and the small tools published under it. It explains what data is collected when you visit, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it.

Social Manager, my personal post-scheduling app, has its own privacy policy. Where a page or tool has its own policy, that policy applies to it.

The site is run by Brandon T. Bande, Gweru, Zimbabwe (“I”, “me”). I aim to follow Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] and the principles of the EU GDPR.

What data is collected

Visit statistics

I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to see which pages are read. For each page view it records:

  • the page address and the page title;
  • the site that linked you here (the referrer), if your browser sends it;
  • your browser and operating system, and your screen size;
  • your country, worked out from your IP address.

GoatCounter does not set cookies, does not store your IP address, and does not track you across other websites. I can't use this data to tell who you are. Blog posts also ask GoatCounter for each post's total view count so it can be shown on the page.

Technical data handled by the services the site runs on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web server, it receives your IP address and request details, and GitHub may keep them in its logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts, so your browser fetches them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

I don't receive or see this data.

Messages you send me

If you contact me on LinkedIn, X, Sessionize or by email, I receive whatever you choose to send, such as your name, profile and message. I only use it to reply to you.

What is not collected

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No cookies, advertising, or tracking pixels from social networks.
  • No sale or sharing of data for marketing.

Why it is used

  • Visit statistics: to understand which writing and talks people find useful. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running and improving the site.
  • Hosting and font data: needed to deliver the pages to your browser.
  • Messages: to answer you, with your consent.

Who it is shared with

Only the services named above (GitHub, Google Fonts and GoatCounter), which process data to run the site. I don't sell, rent or otherwise give your data to anyone else, unless the law requires it. These services may store data outside Zimbabwe, including in the United States and the European Union.

Other websites

The site links to LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Sessionize and many others. Once you follow a link, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Tools on this site

Small tools published under this site run in your browser. If a tool needs to send what you enter to a server, or has its own privacy policy, the tool's page will say so.

How long data is kept

  • Visit statistics: kept in GoatCounter for as long as the site runs. They can't be linked back to you.
  • Messages: kept in the platform you used for as long as the conversation stays there. Ask and I will delete my copy.
  • Hosting and font logs: kept by GitHub and Google under their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask me to tell you what data I hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how it is used. I will reply within 30 days. Because visit statistics can't identify you, in most cases I won't hold anything about you unless you have messaged me.

You can also block GoatCounter with any content blocker, or turn off JavaScript; the site still works without it. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ), the data protection authority, or the authority where you live.

Children

The site is not aimed at children and I don't knowingly collect data about them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, message me on LinkedIn or X (@Rogue0015).
